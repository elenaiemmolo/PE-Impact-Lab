Methodological note. Estimates are based on the Sun & Abraham (2021) staggered difference-in-differences estimator applied to PSM-matched samples.
ATT = Average Treatment Effect on the Treated; whiskers and bands indicate 95% confidence intervals.
In the Heterogeneity view, pale-coloured countries and sectors indicate estimates that are not statistically significant at the 10% level, while colour direction reflects the sign of the point estimate.
Country- and sector-level estimates are shown for groups with at least 10 treated firms (N ≥ 10) in both the Main SME and Full samples. Country borders shown are schematic (Natural Earth, 110m resolution) and for orientation only.
